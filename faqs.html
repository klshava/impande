Clarity & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Finding the right path for your healing journey starts with clarity.

What services does iMpande provide?

iMpande offers organisational consultancy and practice development, tailored training and workshops, speaking engagements, professional supervision and reflective consultation, and individual therapy. Our work connects cultural responsiveness, critical reflection and attention to the contexts and systems that shape people's experiences.

How can iMpande support organisations?

iMpande works with organisations seeking to strengthen culturally responsive practice beyond policy commitments. Consultancy may include practice and resource reviews, cultural consultation, workforce development and reflective conversations about assumptions, systems and barriers. Each engagement is shaped by the organisation's needs and intended outcomes.

What does culturally responsive and decolonial practice mean at iMpande?

We recognise that experiences cannot be understood separately from culture, identity, history, relationships and systems of power. Our approach invites curiosity, reflection and examination of practice, without reducing individuals or communities to assumptions or stereotypes.

Can workshops and training be tailored to our organisation?

Yes. Workshops and training are developed with consideration of the organisation's context, participants and learning intentions. Areas may include cultural responsiveness, identity and belonging, racism, power, critical reflection and culturally responsive engagement.

Is Simangaliso available for speaking engagements?

Yes. Simangaliso Nyoni, Founder and Principal Consultant of iMpande, is available for keynotes, panels and facilitated conversations. Topics may include identity, belonging, race, power, cultural responsiveness and how individual experiences connect with broader systems.

Who is professional supervision suitable for?

Professional supervision and reflective consultation are available to social workers, counsellors and other practitioners seeking space to explore professional challenges, ethics, cultural responsiveness, identity, power and organisational influences on practice. Sessions are tailored to the practitioner's role and learning needs.

Who can access therapy at iMpande?

iMpande offers individual therapy for people exploring identity, belonging, migration, racism, trauma, relationships and life transitions. Therapy is offered in English, isiNdebele and Shona. Please enquire about current appointment arrangements.

What qualifications and experience does the founder have?

Simangaliso Nyoni is a qualified social worker and AASW Member with 17+ years of social work experience across therapeutic practice, leadership, cultural consultation and organisational practice development. She is the Founder and Principal Consultant of iMpande.

How are iMpande's services priced?

Individual therapy is $180 for 50 minutes. Professional supervision is $200 for 60 minutes, and Extended Professional Supervision is $280 for 90 minutes. Consultancy, training, workshops and speaking fees are discussed and agreed at the initial consultation, with the scope and fees confirmed before work begins.

How do I engage iMpande for consultancy, training or speaking?

Please get in touch with an outline of the support you are seeking, the intended outcomes and any relevant timelines. An initial conversation provides an opportunity to clarify the approach, scope, deliverables and fees.

What time zone does iMpande use for bookings?

All iMpande appointment times are based on Melbourne, Victoria, Australia local time (AEST/AEDT). If booking from interstate or overseas, please be mindful of the time difference.
