Clarity & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Finding the right path for your healing journey starts with clarity.

What types of therapy do you offer?

At iMpande, we provide both phone consultations and telehealth services. We specialize in culturally responsive therapy tailored to your unique needs.

What is the cost of therapy?

The cost of our services varies depending on your needs. Individual therapy is priced based on duration. Costs will be discussed during your initial free 30-minute consultation. Please note that we currently do not offer a Medicare rebate.

What are your qualifications?

I am a qualified social worker and counselor with 16 years of experience. I hold both a Masters and a Bachelor of Social Work and am an accredited member of the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW).

What is the difference between counseling and therapy?

While often used interchangeably, counseling generally addresses specific issues over a shorter period, while therapy explores deeper, long-standing patterns. At iMpande, we offer both and tailor our approach to you.

How will I benefit from therapy?

Therapy can help you manage uncomfortable emotions, navigate life transitions, move past trauma, and improve interpersonal relationships. A good match between therapist and client is the most significant predictor of success.
