A Commitment Beyond Individual Healing

iMpande's commitment to healing extends beyond therapeutic and professional spaces. It is also grounded in community, shared responsibility and creating opportunities for others.

Intersecting Stories

In 2018, Simangaliso Nyoni and her dear friend Alyssha collaborated on a writing project that was later published by the Dulwich Centre in March 2020 as part of the book Intersecting Stories . The project also became an opportunity to contribute to something that had long been close to Simangaliso's heart: ensuring girls in Zimbabwe have access to sanitary products.

Supporting Girls in Zimbabwe

When the authors received an advance payment, Alyssha Fooks chose not to take a cent. Instead, the funds were donated towards supporting girls' access to sanitary pads in Zimbabwe. Sadly, in 2024, Alyssha passed away. Her generosity and contribution remain an important part of this story.

This commitment did not begin with the book, nor did it end with that contribution. It continues to inform iMpande's commitment to addressing period poverty and recognising the importance of dignity, access and opportunity for girls.
