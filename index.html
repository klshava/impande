My Essence

The Path to iMpande

My path to healing was not a straight line, but a return to my origins. At 17, driven by my own experiences of navigating spaces where I didn't feel fully understood, I set out to become a counsellor. I saw a gap—a need for support that understood the layers of identity, culture, and generational weight.

Armed with a Masters in Counselling and a Bachelors degree in Social Work, I have dedicated my career to bridging that gap. iMpande is the culmination of years of clinical practice and a deep-seated calling to provide the representation I once sought.

"iMpande means 'root' in isiNdebele. It is a tribute to my khulu (grandfather), Mpande David Nyoni. His wisdom is the bedrock of this practice. We do not just treat symptoms; we look at the soil."
