Professional Standards

Terms & Conditions

Our commitment to ethical practice and professional accountability.

At iMpande, we are committed to providing professional, ethical and culturally responsive services. These terms outline the general arrangements for engaging our services.

1. Our Services

iMpande provides consultancy, training, workshops, speaking engagements, professional supervision and individual therapy. Services are tailored to the needs of individuals, practitioners and organisations.

2. Bookings and Cancellations

Appointments must be arranged in advance. If you need to cancel or reschedule an individual appointment, please provide at least 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations may incur a fee, which will be communicated before booking. All appointment times are based on Melbourne, Victoria, Australia local time (AEST/AEDT).

3. Fees and Payments

Fees for individual therapy and professional supervision are outlined on our Pricing page. For consultancy, training, workshops and speaking engagements, scope, fees and payment arrangements will be discussed and agreed during the initial consultation, before work commences.

4. Privacy and Confidentiality

iMpande respects the privacy and confidentiality of individuals and organisations engaging our services. Personal and professional information is handled in accordance with applicable privacy and professional obligations. Confidentiality may be limited where disclosure is required or authorised by law.

5. Resources and Intellectual Property

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, resources, training materials and original content developed by iMpande remain the intellectual property of iMpande. Materials may not be reproduced, distributed or used beyond their agreed purpose without prior permission.

6. Professional Responsibility

iMpande is committed to ethical, reflective and accountable practice. While services are designed to support learning, development and meaningful change, specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed. Additional terms for organisational engagements will be included in the relevant services agreement.
