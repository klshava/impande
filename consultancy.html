Consultancy

Strengthening culturally responsive practice

iMpande works alongside organisations to strengthen culturally responsive, anti-oppressive and decolonial practice across services, teams and systems.

Request a Consultation

Consultancy may include practice consultation, review of policies and resources, strategic advice and collaborative development of practical recommendations.

Our approach explores the wider context behind presenting challenges, including racism, bias, power, client participation and organisational conditions. We combine specialist expertise with the knowledge of practitioners, leaders and the people affected by services. The aim is to build capability and support responsive practice, not create ongoing dependence on a consultant.

What consultancy can look like

  • Reflective consultation on complex practice situations, including client and family engagement
  • Review of assessments, practice guidance, policies and organisational resources
  • Critical examination of racism, systemic inequities and institutional practices
  • Collaborative recommendations to strengthen communication, participation and service responses
  • Follow-up consultation to integrate new learning and adapt the approach as understanding develops

Our consultation approach

Understand. Examine. Respond. Revisit.

Understand

Explore context, existing work, client perspectives and what is already working.

Examine

Use collaborative inquiry to identify assumptions, gaps, power dynamics and systemic barriers.

Respond

Develop context-informed recommendations and identify responsibilities.

Revisit

Reflect on new information, review progress and adapt practice.

This is an iterative approach, not a checklist.
