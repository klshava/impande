How We Walk Together

Our Services

Our services are built on the principles of social justice, cultural safety, and decolonial practice. We offer a range of pathways for organisations, practitioners and individuals to strengthen culturally responsive, anti-oppressive practice and to heal and grow.

Consultancy

iMpande works alongside organisations to strengthen culturally responsive, anti-oppressive and decolonial practice across services, teams and systems. Consultancy may include practice consultation, review of policies and resources, strategic advice and collaborative development of practical recommendations.

Workshops & Training

iMpande designs and facilitates reflective, participatory workshops grounded in culturally responsive, anti-oppressive and decolonial practice. We recognise that expertise does not give any one person exclusive ownership of knowledge. Our workshops bring specialist knowledge into dialogue with participants’ experiences and practice wisdom, creating opportunities for shared learning, critical reflection and meaningful discussion.

Topics may include cultural responsiveness, racism, racialised experiences, cultural humility, identity, power, intersectionality and systemic inequities. Alongside workshops, iMpande offers tailored training that integrates culturally responsive perspectives and practical application.

Therapy & Counselling

iMpande offers culturally responsive and decolonial therapeutic support grounded in extensive professional experience across mental health, university counselling and sexual assault counselling and advocacy.

Our approach recognises the intersections of mental health, trauma, identity, culture, race and lived experience, creating space for individuals to explore their experiences in ways that honour their dignity, strengths and personal circumstances.

We support individuals navigating emotional and psychological challenges, experiences of trauma, racism, identity, belonging and significant life transitions. Therapeutic services are available in English, isiNdebele and Shona.

  • Language Choice (isiNdebele, Shona, English)
  • Telehealth & Phone Consultations
  • Culturally Safe Environment
