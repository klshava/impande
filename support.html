How We Walk Together

Spaces for Transformation

Our support services are built on the principles of social justice, cultural safety, and decolonial practice. We offer a range of pathways for individuals and organizations to engage in deep healing and growth.

Workshops & Training

I provide workshops for social workers, counsellors, and organisations that foster culturally responsive practices. Through case studies and reflective activities, we explore anti-oppressive and social justice theories to create safer professional environments.

"Simangaliso's approach to workshops is innovative and deeply challenging, pushing us to see beyond traditional Western frameworks."

Speaking Engagements

A trailblazer in multicultural social work, I am available for speaking engagements, including women’s events and expert panels. I share insights on race, power dynamics, and the liberation that comes from honest, validating conversations.

Individual Therapy

Individual therapy is tailored to your needs, prioritising privacy and gently guiding you through your healing journey. At iMpande, we offer the unique choice to participate in therapy in your native language, including **isiNdebele**, **Shona**, and **English**.

  • Language Choice (isiNdebele, Shona, English)
  • Telehealth & Phone Consultations
  • Culturally Safe Environment

Supervision & Reflective Practice

I offer individual supervision to newly graduated social workers and counselors, focusing on the integration of ethical and culturally informed practices. My reflective practice sessions help teams address unconscious bias, racism, and microaggressions to enhance inclusivity.
