The Heart Behind iMpande

My Essence

Simangaliso Nyoni

Founder and Principal Consultant | iMpande

AASW Member

I am a social worker, counsellor and multicultural practice specialist with extensive experience working across complex practice environments.

Through iMpande, I provide consultancy, workshops and training, professional supervision and speaking engagements, supporting practitioners and organisations to strengthen culturally responsive, anti-oppressive and decolonial practice.

My work brings together clinical practice, leadership, strategic consultation and a commitment to critically examining racism, racialised experiences, identity, power and systemic inequities, and how these shape experiences, opportunities and outcomes.

My professional background also includes extensive experience in mental health, university counselling and sexual assault counselling and advocacy, which informs my therapeutic practice and broader consultancy work.

Alongside my consultancy work, I offer culturally responsive and decolonial therapeutic services, recognising the importance of language, identity, belonging and lived experience in healing.

A Legacy of Responsive Care

iMpande, which means 'root' in Isindebele represents the strength and growth that are central to my vision. This name pays homage to my grandfather’s name, Mpande, a pivotal figure whose profound influence has shaped the person I am today. As the cornerstone of my family, his wisdom serves as my compass, guiding my vision to empower women through counselling.

iMpande was born from a recognition that mainstream therapeutic approaches often fail to adequately consider the intersectional experiences of women of colour. While this understanding shaped its beginnings, iMpande has evolved to work across individual, professional and organisational contexts, bringing culturally responsive and decolonial perspectives to therapy, supervision, consultancy, training and speaking engagements.

I am a member of the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW), and I adhere to the highest ethical standards, ensuring that every session is a sanctuary of privacy, respect, and profound transformation.
