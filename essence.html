The Heart Behind iMpande

My Essence

I am Simangaliso Brenda Nyoni, a qualified social worker and counselor with 16 years of experience across the therapeutic landscape. My journey has been defined by a deep commitment to social justice and the decolonization of mental health practices.

Masters of Social Work Bachelor of Social Work

A Legacy of Responsive Care

iMpande, which means "root" in Zulu, was born from a realization that mainstream therapeutic models often fail to capture the intersectional complexities of women of color. My experience as a sexual assault counselor and advocate has taught me that healing is not just an individual act, but a political and cultural one.

I am a member of the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW), and I adhere to the highest ethical standards, ensuring that every session is a sanctuary of privacy, respect, and profound transformation.

Acknowledgement of Country

iMpande positions itself in relationships of accountability to Indigenous people and the land where healing conversations take place. We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the multiple lands across Australia and honor the Elders past and present. Sovereignty was never ceded.
