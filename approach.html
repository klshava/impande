Our Approach

The iMpande Curiosity Cycle

At iMpande, we believe culturally responsive practice begins with curiosity, not assumptions.

The iMpande Curiosity Cycle reflects our commitment to learning, critical reflection and collaborative practice. It encourages us to look beyond what we think we know, consider different perspectives and develop a deeper understanding of people’s experiences.

Grounded in cultural humility, the cycle brings together four interconnected practices:

Notice

See what is happening without assumptions or judgement.

Be curious

Ask questions and stay open to different perspectives.

Meaning making

Listen deeply and create shared understanding.

Respond

Act with intention and humility, in partnership.

The cycle recognises that cultural responsiveness is not a destination or a checklist. As new knowledge and perspectives emerge, our understanding and responses must continue to evolve.

At its heart, the Curiosity Cycle is an invitation to remain open, reflective and responsive in how we engage with others.
