Magazine Feature · 2025

HER Story Diversity Connector

HER Story is a global, collaborative initiative that brings together women from all cultures and backgrounds to share personal stories that inspire, inform and empower others. Founded by global curator Gertrude Matshe, the initiative includes HER Story Diversity Connector magazine, in which Simangaliso Nyoni was interviewed and featured in 2025.
